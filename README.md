### Householder Transformation
- Householder transformation/matrix plays very
important rules in wide range of applications such as finding
eigenvalues of matrices and solving systems of linear equations.
- For finding eigenvalues, Householder matrices can be used to
reduce a symmetric matrix to a similar tridiagonal matrix or a
general matrix to a similar upper Hessenberg matrix.
- For solving Ax = b, Householder matrices can be used to find a
QR factorization of A, A = QR, where Q is orthogonal and is a
product of Householder matrices, and R is upper triangular. The
role of QR factorization is similar to that of LU factorization.
